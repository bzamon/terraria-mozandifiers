# MOZANDIFIERS INDEX

Maintainer note: architecture or naming changes should update `MOZANDIFIERS_CONTEXT.md` and `MOZANDIFIERS_INDEX.md` in the same task.

| Prefix / System | Fantasy | Roll gate | Core stat shape | Runtime owners | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Attuned` | Magic resonance that culminates in a free empowered cast | Any mana-using magic weapon | `0.84x` damage, `0.9x` knockback, `0.88x` use time, `1.1x` shoot speed, `0.75x` mana cost | `AttunedPrefix.cs`, `AttunedPlayer.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalItem.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Hit and cast both build resonance; empowered casts open a short projectile bonus window |
| `Awakened` | Sword sweet-spot meter that enters a timed awakened state and then recovery | Standard sword-like swing melee only | Dormant use-speed bonus and crit bonus; awakened damage/use-speed/scale are runtime state bonuses | `AwakenedPrefix.cs`, `AwakenedPlayer.cs`, `AwakenedCombatHelper.cs`, `AwakenedRuntime.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalNPC.cs` | Item-side only by design; boss bonus damage is budgeted per player |
| `Breaching` | Heavy siege modifier for armor breaking and impact feedback | Heavy swing melee, heavy projectile melee, or forceful ranged projectile weapons | Damage, knockback, faster use, crit penalty, armor penetration, optional scale | `BreachingPrefix.cs`, `BreachingFeedbackPlayer.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalItem.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Owns the old `Colossal` size fantasy through config compatibility |
| `Catalytic` | Magic mark and consume loop | Standard magic projectile weapons | `0.9x` damage, `-2` crit | `CatalyticPrefix.cs`, `CatalyticRuntime.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalNPC.cs` | Mark and arm timers are per NPC per player |
| `Deadeye` | Charged ranged shot with crit burst payoff | Standard ranged projectile weapons | Faster use, faster projectiles, lower knockback, `+6` crit | `DeadeyePrefix.cs`, `DeadeyePlayer.cs`, `DeadeyeRuntime.cs`, `DeadeyeBurstProjectile.cs` | Standing still can charge faster; one projectile consumes each ready shot |
| `Desperate` | Low-life damage ramp with surge hits at critical life | Standard melee, ranged, and magic combat weapons | `0.9x` damage, configured crit bonus | `DesperatePrefix.cs`, `DesperatePlayer.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalItem.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Threshold visuals and surge timing are player-local |
| `Fractured` | Projectile cascade into unstable fracture copies | `Echoing` modes implemented by `WeaponPrefix.IsImplementedEchoingMode(...)` | `0.95x` damage, `1.12x` use time, `-8` crit | `EchoingPrefix.cs`, `FracturedRuntime.cs`, `WeaponPrefix.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Internal file/class naming still says `Echoing` |
| `Radiant` | Direct-hit burn and light | Standard direct combat weapons | `0.96x` damage, `0.95x` knockback, `+2` crit | `RadiantPrefix.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalItem.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Applies `On Fire!`; no extra persistent state |

| `Shifting` | Rotating simulated stronger modifier state while in combat | Only swing melee, ranged projectile, and magic projectile weapons supported by `ShiftingSimulationPools` | No direct base stat line; simulated modifier values are scaled by config strength | `ShiftingPrefix.cs`, `ShiftingPlayer.cs`, `ShiftingSimulationPools.cs`, `ShiftingSimulationDisplay.cs`, `ShiftingSimulationStatScaling.cs`, `ShiftingSimulationEffectScaling.cs`, `Mozandifiers.cs` | Server-authoritative with explicit sync packet |
| `Skirmishing` | Hit to open mobility window, then spend it on a stronger follow-up shot | Ranged projectile weapons | `0.9x` damage, `0.85x` knockback, `0.8x` use time, `1.2x` shoot speed, `+4` crit | `SkirmishingPrefix.cs`, `SkirmishingPlayer.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalItem.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Follow-up bonus is projectile-tag based |
| `Spinbound` | Golden-ratio shot geometry and Fibonacci cadence bonus | Ranged projectile weapons | `1.08x` damage, `1.12x` use time, `0.92x` knockback, `1.12x` shoot speed, `-4` crit, `+6` armor penetration | `SpinboundPrefix.cs`, `SpinboundPlayer.cs`, `SpinboundRuntime.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Golden Spin tiers are consumed once per projectile hit |
| `Stormforged` | Proc-based chain lightning | Swing melee and standard projectile combat weapons | `0.96x` damage, `1.06x` use time, `+4` crit | `StormforgedPrefix.cs`, `StormforgedChainHelper.cs`, `StormforgedPlayer.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalItem.cs`, `WeaponPrefixProjectileDispatch.cs` | Chain recursion is explicitly suppressed |
| `Temporal` | Fast tempo that stores damage into a delayed collapse once pressure fractures the target | Any standard weapon | `TemporalPrefix.SetStats(...)` directly assigns attack-speed/use-time/projectile-speed behavior | `TemporalPrefix.cs`, `TemporalRuntime.cs`, `TemporalFeedbackPlayer.cs`, `WeaponPrefixGlobalNPC.cs` | Most target-state-heavy modifier after `Shifting` |
| `PrefixTuningConfig` | Server-side balance surface for every live prefix | N/A | Exposes all config-backed values and legacy compatibility shims | `Common/Config/PrefixTuningConfig.cs` | Includes legacy `Echoing` and `Colossal` property aliases |
