Adds custom weapon prefixes to Terraria through tModLoader, with two included bonus vanity pets based on my real dogs.

Current version includes:
- 14 weapon prefixes: Fractured, Attuned, Awakened, Breaching, Skirmishing, Vampiric, Radiant, Stormforged, Temporal, Desperate, Catalytic, Deadeye, Spinbound, and Shifting
- 2 bonus vanity dog pets: Amelia and Ludovica
- 4 server-side mod config sliders: Fracture I/II/III chance and Breaching heavy size
- Recent reworks for Fractured, Deadeye, Temporal, Vampiric, Skirmishing, and Awakened
