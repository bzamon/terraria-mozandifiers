Adds custom weapon prefixes to Terraria through tModLoader, with two included bonus vanity pets based on my real dogs.

Current version includes:
- 14 weapon prefixes: Echoing, Attuned, Breaching, Skirmishing, Sanguine, Radiant, Stormforged, Temporal, Desperate, Siphoning, Catalytic, Headshot, Spinbound, and Shifting
- 2 bonus vanity dog pets: Amelia and Ludovica
- 2 server-side mod config sliders: Echo chance and Breaching heavy size
