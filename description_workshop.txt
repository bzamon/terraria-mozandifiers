[h1]Mozan's Arcane Modifiers[/h1]

Adds custom weapon prefixes for Terraria with clear tradeoffs and configurable tuning, plus two included bonus vanity pets based on my real dogs.

[b]Current features:[/b]
- 14 custom weapon prefixes: Fractured, Attuned, Awakened, Breaching, Skirmishing, Vampiric, Radiant, Stormforged, Temporal, Desperate, Catalytic, Deadeye, Spinbound, and Shifting
- 2 bonus vanity dog pets: Amelia and Ludovica
- Fractured can split shots into unstable dimensional copies with reduced damage
- Vampiric steals life on hit, lets magic weapons siphon mana, and can enter Frenzy against dying prey
- Catalytic rewards follow-up magic hits with bonus damage
- Deadeye rewards patient ranged shots with a charged precision round and leafy crit bursts
- Spinbound empowers Fibonacci-timed shots and can trigger Golden Spin amplification
- Shifting periodically rewrites itself into stronger temporary modifier states while in combat
- Breaching now carries the heavy siege size bonus on slow swing weapons
- 4 server-side mod config sliders for Fracture I/II/III chance and Breaching heavy size

[b]Current scope:[/b]
Focused on weapon prefixes, with Amelia and Ludovica included as bonus vanity pets. Accessory and armor prefixes are not implemented.
