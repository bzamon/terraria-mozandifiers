[h1]Mozan's Arcane Modifiers[/h1]

Adds a first wave of custom weapon prefixes for Terraria with clear tradeoffs and configurable tuning.

[b]Current features:[/b]
- 5 custom weapon prefixes: Colossal, Echoing, Attuned, Breaching, and Skirmishing
- Echoing can fire an extra echo shot
- Colossal increases weapon size and power at the cost of speed
- Mod configuration sliders for Echo chance and Colossal size

[b]Current scope:[/b]
Focused on weapon prefixes only. Accessory and armor prefixes are not implemented yet.
